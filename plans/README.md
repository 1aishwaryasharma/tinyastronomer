### Sky Tonight

- **Date label is a day off in Samoa, Tonga, Wallis.**
  `public/sky-forecast.js:58-62` (`localNoon`) derives noon from longitude,
  not the place's clock; at UTC+13 with longitude −172° the "night of" a date
  is the following evening. Plan 001 makes the right night show; the label
  stays wrong. Fixing it changes `localNoon`, which `REVIEW.md` records as
  settled — needs the owner's decision. M, MED risk.
- **A stored device location keeps the device's current clock after travel.**
  `public/sky-location.js:128,147-151,296`: no zone is saved with a `geo`
  fix. S.
- **"Use my location" can stay disabled if the permission prompt is never
  answered.** `public/sky-tonight.html:949-952`,
  `public/sky-location.js:138-158`. Browser-dependent; investigate first. S.
- **Manual "Set" awaits the 952 KB place list with no feedback and reads the
  inputs after the wait.** `public/sky-tonight.html:961-966`. S.
- **"times in <zone>" note compares offsets at the current moment, not on
  the chosen date.** `public/sky-tonight.html:577-579`. S.
- **No way to clear the saved location** (`localStorage['sky.location']`),
  which matters on shared school devices. `public/sky-location.js:31-37`. S.
