✦ The big idea Seasons are not because Earth gets closer to the Sun. They happen because Earth is tilted, so each half leans toward the Sun for part of the year and away for the rest.
Teaching modelAccuracy & sources

Earth’s 23.4° tilt and its fixed direction in space are represented. The orbit is drawn as a circle, sizes and distances are compressed, and the four season markers divide the year evenly. This shows astronomical seasons and immediate sunlight geometry, not local weather or delayed heating. Actual equinox and solstice times vary by year and time zone.
