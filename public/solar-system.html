Planet facts use published reference values. The animation uses simplified circular mean orbits; starting positions are illustrative, spin is not time-scaled, and moon sizes and distances are visual aids. The default view compresses sizes and distances. In Distance View, planet sizes are proportional to one another and mean orbital spacing is proportional, but size and distance still use different scales.
